Soft Tissue Tumors

01 · Recognition rules

  • Soft-tissue sarcoma: often slow-growing + painless; mostly adults; extremities most common.
  • Concerning lesion: deep / large / growing; proximal extremity > golf ball or hand/foot > marble → image (lecture rule).
  • Spread: blood → lungs; lymph-node spread rare (~5%).
  • Poor outcome: big + deep + high grade + metastasis.
  • Biopsy: sarcoma-team planning; longitudinal tract, meticulous hemostasis, drain in incision line.
  • Grade: differentiation + mitoses + necrosis; margins → local recurrence.

03 · Fat + fibrous tumors

  • Lipoma: mobile, superficial, bland fat, yellow/greasy; excision cures.
  • Liposarcoma: adult, deep thigh/retroperitoneum, large mass.
  • Well-differentiated/ALT: atypical adipocytic stroma → MDM2/CDK4 amplification.
  • Myxoid: lipoblasts + chicken-wire vessels + myxoid background → t(12;16) FUS–DDIT3.
  • Palmar fibromatosis: Dupuytren → flexion contractures; plantar: Ledderhose; penile: Peyronie.
  • Desmoid: abdominal wall/mesentery; bland fibroblasts + collagen; infiltrates, recurs, never metastasizes; β-catenin pathway.

04 · Muscle tumors

  • Leiomyoma: uterine fibroid, whorled, blunt-ended cigar nuclei, bland mitoses.
  • Leiomyosarcoma: arises de novo; smooth-muscle spindle morphology + pleomorphism/mitoses/necrosis.
  • Cardiac rhabdomyoma: pediatric + tuberous sclerosis; oral/head-and-neck rhabdomyoma: adult.
  • Embryonal RMS: pediatric GU/head-neck; botryoid grapes, cambium layer, strap/tadpole rhabdomyoblasts; better prognosis.
  • Alveolar RMS: young adult deep thigh, alveolar morphology, t(2;13) PAX3–FOXO (lecture notation); poorer prognosis.

05 · Uncertain differentiation

  • Synovial sarcoma: 20–50 years, deep near knee; not of synovial origin.
  • Biphasic: spindle + glandular epithelial; monophasic may lack obvious glands.
  • t(X;18) → SS18–SSX; aggressive → lung/bone metastases.
  • Undifferentiated STS: listed in classification, older adult association; no further diagnostic profile supplied by these slides.

07 · First Aid rapid connections

  • Desmoid → Gardner/FAP (APC pathway) → abdominal desmoid with polyposis.
  • Cardiac rhabdomyoma → tuberous sclerosis → childhood intracardiac masses.
  • Embryonal RMS → sarcoma botryoides → grape-like pediatric GU lesion.
  • Alveolar RMS → PAX3–FOXO1 pathway → deep extremity lesion.
  • Synovial sarcoma → SS18–SSX → young adult peri-knee mass.

08 · Rapid-fire recall

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Classic metastatic site for soft-tissue sarcomas?

Lungs, via hematogenous spread.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Three components of FNCLCC histologic grade?

Differentiation, mitotic count, necrosis.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Characteristic vessels of myxoid liposarcoma?

Delicate branching chicken-wire capillaries.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Gene amplification in well-differentiated liposarcoma?

MDM2 and/or CDK4.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · What makes fibromatosis intermediate?

Infiltration/recurrence without metastases.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Smooth-muscle spindle-cell nuclear shape?

Blunt-ended cigar-shaped nuclei.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Grape-like pediatric urinary mass?

Embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma, botryoid type.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Fusion in synovial sarcoma?

SS18–SSX from t(X;18).

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Syndrome with cardiac rhabdomyoma?

Tuberous sclerosis.

RETRIEVAL CHECKPOINT · Does leiomyosarcoma arise from leiomyoma?

No, de novo.
