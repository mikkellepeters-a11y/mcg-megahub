Introduction to Rheumatologic Disease — Factoid

MSK-Skin · Week 15 · Hoffer · Original Week 14 compact two-column factoid format
Anchor set: Articular = active + passive pain · >1 hr inflammatory stiffness vs ≤30 min mechanical · mono 1 / oligo ≤4 / poly >4 · RA MCP/PIP/wrist vs OA DIP/PIP/1st CMC · anti-CCP specific · pannus → erosions · Felty triad · methotrexate weekly.

Pain localization

  • Articular: entire joint line; active ≈ passive ROM painful; effusion = arthritis.
  • Periarticular: focal tenderness; selected movements; active > passive ROM.
  • Nonarticular: diffuse / not tied to ROM; neurologic referred = paresthesia or reflex loss.

Inflammatory vs noninflammatory

  • Inflammatory: >1 hr stiffness, improves with activity, worsens at rest; often ↑ ESR/CRP, fatigue.
  • Noninflammatory: ≤30 min stiffness, worsens with activity, improves with rest; minimal systemic signs.
  • Boggy fusiform swelling = synovitis; bony enlargement / crepitus = OA.

Synovial fluid WBC

  • Normal <200/mm³; noninflammatory 200–2,000.
  • Inflammatory 2,000–50,000, PMNs >50%.
  • Septic >50,000, PMNs >75%; often purulent.
  • Send cell count/diff, crystals, Gram stain, aerobic + anaerobic culture.

Crystals

  • MSU: needle; strongly negative birefringence → gout.
  • CPPD: rhomboid/rod; weakly positive → pseudogout/CPPD.
  • Cholesterol: notched rectangular plates; steroid crystals after injection.

Describe an arthritis pattern

  • Timing: acute <6 weeks, chronic >6 weeks; intermittent, migratory, additive.
  • Number: mono = 1; oligo = ≤4; poly = >4.
  • Assess symmetric/asymmetric, small vs large, axial and specific joint distribution.

OA hallmarks

  • Mechanical cartilage loss → subchondral thickening/eburnation and osteophytes.
  • DIP (Heberden), PIP (Bouchard), 1st CMC, knee, hip, spine; typically spares MCPs/wrists.
  • X-ray: osteophytes, asymmetric narrowing, subchondral sclerosis/cysts.
  • Erosive OA: central erosion + lateral osteophytes = gull wing.

RA hallmarks

  • Chronic, persistent, symmetric polyarthritis; morning stiffness >1 hr, improves with use.
  • MCP, PIP, wrists; may involve MTP, ankles, knees; typically spares DIPs, 1st CMC.
  • Boggy synovitis, fusiform swelling; marginal erosions + periarticular osteopenia.
  • Only spinal involvement taught: C1–C2.

RA immunology / risk

  • Female:male 3:1; peak incidence 4th–6th decades.
  • Smoking, periodontal disease, microbiome + HLA-DRB1 shared epitope (*04, *01).
  • PAD converts arginine → citrulline → loss of tolerance / ACPA.
  • Synovitis → pannus → proteases + osteoclasts → cartilage/bone erosions.

RF vs anti-CCP

  • RF = IgM against IgG Fc; 60–80% sensitivity; 80–86% specificity; nonspecific.
  • Anti-CCP/ACPA: 57–67% sensitivity; 93–99% specificity.
  • RF can appear with hepatitis C, endocarditis and other autoimmune diseases.
  • RA remains clinical; antibody negative does not exclude RA.

RA hand deformities

  • Ulnar drift at MCPs from capsular/tendon damage.
  • Swan neck: PIP hyperextension, DIP flexion.
  • Boutonnière: PIP flexion, DIP hyperextension.
  • Z-thumb: 1st MCP flexion, thumb IP hyperextension.

Atlantoaxial instability

  • RA synovitis → transverse-ligament laxity → C1–C2 subluxation.
  • >3 mm abnormal per lecture; possible cord/vertebral artery injury.
  • Neck pain, Lhermitte-like shocks, drop attacks, syncope.
  • Consider flexion/extension C-spine imaging with symptoms and before surgery/intubation (lecture guidance).

Rheumatoid nodules

  • About 25% of RA; mobile subcutaneous nodules at extensor forearm/pressure points.
  • Histology: palisading histiocytes + central fibrinoid necrosis.
  • Do not confuse with fixed hard OA Heberden/Bouchard nodes.

Extra-articular RA

  • Pulmonary nodules; Caplan = nodules + pneumoconiosis; ILD and pleural effusions.
  • Eye: episcleritis (mild) vs scleritis (deep severe pain) vs peripheral ulcerative keratitis.
  • Felty: RA + neutropenia + splenomegaly.
  • Increased coronary artery disease risk from inflammation.

Methotrexate essentials

  • First-line when appropriate; 15–25 mg every 7 days, oral or subQ.
  • Inhibits AICAR transformylase at RA doses → ↑ adenosine.
  • Give daily folic acid.
  • Nausea, stomatitis, leukopenia, hair loss, hepatotoxicity; serious teratogenicity, pneumonitis.
