Superficial: PT, FCR, PL, FCU. Intermediate: FDS. Deep: FDP, FPL, PQ. The superficial layer shares the common flexor attachment on the medial epicondyle (additional ulnar head for PT/FCU).

FDS = PIP (middle phalanges); FDP = DIP (distal phalanges); FPL = thumb IP. FCR = radial deviation; FCU = ulnar deviation.
