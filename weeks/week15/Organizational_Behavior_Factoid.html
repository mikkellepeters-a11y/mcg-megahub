Organizational Behavior, Culture & Environmental Factors — Factoid

MSK-Skin · Week 15 · Health Systems Science · Wilkins · Compact Week 14 format
Anchor set: psychological safety = interpersonal risks · WalkRounds + feedback · tiered huddles · evidence-based design · decentralized stations → 55% fewer falls in lecture example · safety culture + standardization · FMEA before / RCA after · CUSP.

Core organizational factors

  • Leadership structure; communication norms; resource allocation; organizational culture; psychological safety; standardization and safety culture.

Psychological safety

  • Feeling safe to take interpersonal risks → speak up, report errors, share ideas.
  • Not conflict avoidance or job security.
  • ICU dosage discrepancy: junior resident's escalation permits correction.

Leadership WalkRounds

  • Leaders listen → log concerns → act → give feedback.
  • Survey of 829 healthcare settings: feedback associated with higher safety culture and engagement, lower burnout, better work–life balance.

Culture and patient outcomes

  • Positive culture associated with fewer deaths, infections, better satisfaction.
  • Lecture review: 74% of studies reported positive or mixed positive/no associations.
  • Toxic hierarchy may suppress reporting.

Resilience and burnout

  • Poor support, communication and moral distress contribute to burnout.
  • Resilience requires foresight, coping and recovery at individual, team and organizational levels.

Communication tools

  • Tiered huddles: frontline → unit → leadership.
  • Boundary spanners connect levels.
  • SBAR = Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation.
  • I-PASS and TeamSTEPPS support structured teamwork.

Handoff case

  • Nurses developed SBAR/I-PASS templates but feared criticism and did not implement.
  • Barrier = lack of psychological safety, not absence of technical knowledge.

Evidence-based design

  • Research guides layout, lighting, acoustics, materials.
  • Include frontline staff in decisions and monitor outcomes after redesign.
  • Built environment influences safety, experience and efficiency.

Single-patient rooms

  • Reduced cross-contamination opportunities; improved privacy/confidentiality; lower noise and better sleep.

Decentralized stations

  • Shorter trips, visibility, patient access, less fatigue.
  • Central corridors up to 150 ft versus maximum 54 ft to decentralized station.
  • Steps per shift 8,362–9,422 vs mean 8,093.

Falls and visibility

  • Falls common during unassisted transfers.
  • Clear sightlines and staff proximity permit timely help.
  • Study example: 51 → 23 falls in 3 months = 55% reduction after relocation.

Room and workplace stressors

  • Bed and sink placement affect perceptions and workflow.
  • Noise, clutter and poor lighting raise errors and stress.
  • Ergonomic design reduces staff injury.

Infection control through design

  • HEPA filters + airflow direction reduce airborne exposure; sink placement supports hand hygiene.
  • Construction/renovation: ventilation and filtration limit spread of infectious particles.

Safety culture foundations

  • Shared values, beliefs and norms prioritizing safety.
  • Components: leadership commitment, teamwork, just culture (non-punitive reporting), learning culture, patient-centeredness.

Standardization

  • Consistent evidence-based practices → reduced variation and preventable errors.
  • Checklists and care bundles need teamwork and a climate where concerns are raised.

FMEA vs RCA

  • FMEA = prospective analysis of what might fail.
  • RCA = retrospective investigation of an event's contributing causes.
  • Checklists = consistent completion of key actions.

CUSP

  • Comprehensive Unit-Based Safety Program combines technical + adaptive strategies: identify issues → educate on safety science → engage executives → implement evidence-based interventions → measure/share outcomes.

Children’s Hospitals SPS

  • 33 hospitals used safety bundles plus culture interventions.
  • Reported serious safety events ↓ 50%; hospital-acquired conditions ↓ up to 71%.
