Exam traps & mechanisms

Beighton: 9-point hypermobility assessment; professor notes score ≥5 currently or historically supports hypermobility. Marfan mechanism: defective fibrillin microfibrils + increased TGF-β signaling → reduced elastic support and overgrowth.

Classical EDS: Papyraceous scars. Vascular EDS: Organ or arterial rupture. Marfan: Fibrillin-1, aortic root and lens.

02 · Fibromyalgia: recognize & manage

Fibromyalgia recognition

  • Core triad: widespread pain + fatigue + nonrestorative sleep; often brain fog and exertional flares.
  • Mechanism: central sensitization → allodynia → nociplastic pain amplification.
  • Exam: diffuse tender points, no objective effusions/deformity or neurologic deficits.
  • Testing: clinical diagnosis, usually normal CBC/chemistry, ESR/CRP, CK, TSH; no diagnostic imaging/autoantibody test.

Testing & management

  • Triggers: trauma, infection, sleep apnea, inflammatory rheumatic disease, PTSD, stress.
  • First line: education, aerobic exercise, sleep work, CBT; second line: duloxetine, pregabalin, milnacipran.
  • Avoid as primary therapies: opioids and NSAIDs (lecture summary).

03 · CRPS: trauma → regional autonomic pain

CRPS presentation

  • Trigger: fracture, sprain, surgery (often one limb); often develops within 3 months.
  • Core presentation: burning regional pain, allodynia/hyperalgesia, red/blue changes, altered temperature, edema, hyperhidrosis and reduced ROM.
  • Acute: swollen, color/temperature changes; bone scan increased uptake.

Phases & management

  • Dystrophic: cool/moist, cyanosis, sparse hair, weak limb; imaging not helpful in lecture.
  • Atrophic: glossy/atrophic skin, nail changes, dystonia, loss of function; X-ray patchy osteoporosis.
  • Treatment: multidisciplinary PT and CBT; selected IV ketamine, antiosteoporotics, stimulation; early-only steroids per lecturer.

FIRST AID RAPID CONNECTIONS

First Aid rapid connections

  1. OI: COL1A1/COL1A2 → defective type I collagen → brittle bone. FA p. 729; objective gap
  2. EDS: faulty collagen synthesis → stretchy skin, joint hypermobility, bruising. FA p. 729
  3. Marfan: FBN1 → fibrillin microfibrils → multisystem elastic tissue weakness. FA p. 729
  4. Ectopia lentis: Marfan versus homocystinuria are important associations. FA p. 571
  5. Fibromyalgia: nonrestorative sleep, cognitive disturbance and normal ESR. FA p. 498

Recall promptAnswer
Which collagen is defective in classical EDS?Type V collagen; COL5A1/COL5A2.
What separates hypermobile from classical EDS on the skin exam?Absence of classical wide, papyraceous scars.
Which EDS type causes organ rupture?Vascular EDS: type III collagen (COL3A1).
Which protein is mutated in Marfan?Fibrillin-1 (FBN1), chromosome 15.
What are the 3 main Marfan systems?Skeletal, ocular and cardiovascular.
What mechanism explains fibromyalgia allodynia?Central sensitization / nociplastic pain.
