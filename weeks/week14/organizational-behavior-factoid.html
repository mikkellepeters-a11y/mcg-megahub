One-minute framework

Leadership + communication + resources + culture + psychological safety + standardization → teamwork, reporting, workflow reliability and patient outcomes.

Objective 1 · Organizational factors

Psychological safetySafe to take interpersonal risks → speak up, report mistakes, share ideas.
Leadership WalkRounds + feedbackHigher safety culture and engagement; lower burnout (observational association).
Safety continuumBlame → neutral → learning culture.
Resilience levelsIndividual coping → team communication → organizational learning.
Tiered huddlesFrontline → unit → leadership.
Structured handoffSBAR / I-PASS / TeamSTEPPS help reduce information loss.
WalkRounds study in the lecture: 829 healthcare settings. Feedback was associated with ↑ safety scores, ↑ engagement, ↑ work–life balance, ↓ burnout.

Objective 2 · Workplace design

DesignMechanism → effect
Single-patient roomLess cross-contamination, more privacy, less noise, better sleep.
Decentralized stationsBetter sightlines and proximity → faster access, less walking, fewer falls.
DaylightBetter mood and alertness; potentially fewer errors.
HEPA / airflow / sink placementImproved airborne and contact infection control.
Ergonomic layoutLess physical strain and fewer staff injuries.
Poor light / noise / clutterDistraction, stress and error risk.
Nurse station redesign in lecture: 51 → 23 falls in 3 months (55% reduction); centralized corridors up to 150 ft vs decentralized station distance up to 54 ft.

Objective 3 · Safety and standardization

Safety cultureShared norms that prioritize patient safety, reporting and learning.
StandardizationConsistent evidence-based processes → reduced unnecessary variation.
FMEAProspective: what could fail?
RCARetrospective: why did harm occur?
CUSPTechnical + adaptive strategies: safety science, leadership, intervention, measurement.
Just cultureFair accountability + ability to report and learn.
Children’s Hospitals’ SPS Collaborative: 33 hospitals; 50% fewer serious safety events; hospital-acquired condition reductions up to 71%.

Don't mix these up

ABDifference
Psychological safetyAvoiding conflictSpeaking up even with disagreement.
Blame cultureLearning culturePunishes error vs uses reports for improvement.
FMEARCABefore vs after an event.
CultureProtocolBehavioral norms vs standardized action.

Rapid-fire recall

1. Definition of psychological safety?

Feeling safe to take interpersonal risks.

2. What must follow Leadership WalkRounds?

Action on concerns and feedback to staff.

3. Communication chain of tiered huddles?

Frontline → unit → leadership.

4. What design limits cross-contamination?

Single-patient rooms.

5. What redesign feature can reduce falls?

Decentralized nurse stations with direct visibility.

6. What percent fall reduction was reported?

55% (51 to 23 over three months).

7. FMEA is performed when?

Prospectively, before failure.

8. RCA is performed when?

After an event, to identify contributing causes.

9. What does CUSP combine?

Technical and adaptive safety strategies.

10. How many hospitals participated in SPS collaborative?

33 hospitals.

Source: Wilkins Session 2 course slides. Reported associations and before/after results are not guarantees of causation.
